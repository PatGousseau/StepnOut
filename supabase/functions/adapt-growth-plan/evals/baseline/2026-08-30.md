Regressions found and fixed while establishing this baseline:

- A report could emit the journal-only implicit-completion flag. The
  report-specific schema now fixes that flag to `false`.
- A valid next step could carry a mismatched response label. Response type is
  now derived from the structured fields rather than trusting the redundant
  label.
- Repeated opportunity evidence could change the next step while leaving a
  contradicted blocker formulation persistent. That evidence pattern now
  requires a confirmation-gated plan revision.
- A cancelled meeting could receive reflection only and leave no active step.
  One-time no-opportunity reports now preserve or reschedule the relevant
  experiment.
