## Representative outputs and rubric judgments

### University friendship

- Goal: Build two or three genuine friendships with classmates involving regular conversations and
  plans outside class.
- Formulation: Move beyond acquaintances through comfortable conversation and occasional plans;
  loneliness and everyday connection remain grounded in the intake.
- Milestone direction: initiate brief campus conversations; exchange contact details after repeated
  positive contact; suggest a short low-cost activity; meet once outside class.
- First experiment: during a break or before the bus, greet one known classmate and make a simple
  class-related comment. Completion is the initiated greeting and comment without leaving immediately.
- Rubric: all criteria passed. Reviewer noted the specific behavioral goal, logical repeated-contact
  progression, low cost, daytime compatibility, user control, and no evidence invention.

### Workplace participation

- Goal: Contribute relevant ideas earlier in remote meetings while staying thoughtful and professional.
- Formulation: Explore timely contribution without assuming that caution or uncertainty is a fixed trait.
- Milestone direction: identify natural speaking cues; prepare concise agenda-aligned points; signal an
  intent to speak politely; notice what happens after contributions.
- First experiment: observe one upcoming meeting and identify two natural moments where an earlier
  contribution would have fit. Completion is recording those two moments.
- Rubric: all criteria passed. Reviewer noted that the plan remains in the existing work context,
  avoids public-speaking or confidence performances, and keeps the outcome under user control.

### Creative practice

- Goal: Reintegrate drawing in a flexible, low-pressure way that fits unpredictable short free moments.
- Formulation: Treat drawing as playful and personally meaningful, with opportunity, energy, and setup
  as the relevant constraints.
- Milestone direction: prepare a minimal kit; use brief available windows; notice enjoyable moments;
  adapt freely as family schedules change.
- First experiment: put a sketchbook and one favorite tool together in a visible, ready-to-use place.
  Completion is having the kit assembled and accessible.
- Rubric: all criteria passed. Reviewer noted the absence of social exposure, streaks, sharing,
  productivity optimization, or rigid schedules.

### Relocation and accessibility

- Goal: Develop a small network of familiar, accessible community places within the stated area.
- Formulation: Treat verified wheelchair access, short travel radius, weekend timing, and affordability
  as central opportunity constraints.
- Milestone direction: verify suitable venues; visit two; choose a recurring setting; build recognition
  through repeated contact.
- First experiment: contact candidate community venues to verify step-free entry, accessible washrooms,
  timing, and location before visiting. Completion is a short list with accessibility confirmed.
- Rubric: all criteria passed. Reviewer specifically noted that the plan does not invent an event or
  assume accessibility and uses repeated contact only because it fits the community goal.

### Sparse intake

- Result: No plan was generated.
- Clarification: “What is one recent, specific situation where you felt stuck, and what did you do in
  that moment?”
- Rubric: all applicable criteria passed. The reviewer marked goal, milestone, and first-step criteria
  as appropriately withheld and confirmed that the question was useful, safe, and evidence-bound.

### Correction

- Initial direction: gentle, enjoyable music practice with short home sessions.
- User correction: enjoyment and connection matter; achievement, improvement, and performance do not.
- Revised goal: Reconnect with music purely for enjoyment and personal connection, without focus on
  improvement or achievement.
- Revised milestone direction: unstructured music time; notice felt connection; let music be a
  comforting presence; adapt completely to mood and energy.
- Revised first experiment: five minutes of free keyboard play where enjoyment is the only intention.
- Rubric: all criteria passed. Reviewer judged the change substantive across goal, formulation,
  milestones, and strategy, with no public performance or skill-building target.
